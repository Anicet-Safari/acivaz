# Acivaz

Turn your trial balance into a professional financial reporting pack.

**Open the app:** https://anicet-safari.github.io/acivaz/

Prepare draft financial statements, notes and management reports. Review account mappings, resolve checks, and export to PDF or Excel, all in your browser. Acivaz is built for accountants and bookkeepers in Rwanda.

## The workflow

**Clients → Reporting period → Import → Map → Adjust → Review → Export**

- **Clients and periods.** Each client has its own reporting periods. A period keeps its own trial balance, settings, adjustments, disclosures, commentary, auditor's report, review status and report versions. Mappings are saved for the client, so the next period maps itself. A new period can roll forward the settings and disclosures from the last one.
- **Import.** Load a trial balance from Excel or CSV (Tally, QuickBooks, Sage, Odoo or your own workbook), or send figures from Books.
- **Map.** Put each account on a line of the reports.
- **Adjust.** Post balanced adjusting entries on top of the import. The import is never changed, and the adjusted trial balance shows the imported, adjustment and adjusted amounts.
- **Review.** Each period is **Draft**, **Needs review** or **Finalised**. Acivaz checks the trial balance, mapping, balance sheet and cash flow. The reviewer then confirms the mappings, reconciliations, adjustments, disclosures and approval. If anything changes after finalising, the period goes back to Draft. Every step is kept in the review history.
- **Export.** Financial statements, management report or the full pack, as PDF and Excel. Until a period is finalised, every page is marked DRAFT. Each download is recorded as a numbered version.

What the reports contain:

- **Financial statements** in the IFRS for SMEs format: profit or loss, financial position, changes in equity and cash flows, with numbered notes, accounting policies, year-end disclosures, a directors' report and an approval page (Law N° 007/2021 governing companies), plus cash flow workings for the reviewer
- **Management report**: key figures, charts that explain each movement, a ratio scorecard and a commentary draft

**More tools:** Books (sales, purchases, bank and VAT under RRA rules), a three-year forecast in three scenarios, a monthly budget, a valuation, loan and project schedules, and a business plan.

Acivaz prepares and checks the pack. Professional review, signing by the directors and any assessment of compliance remain separate steps.

## Your data

Files are read and processed in the browser on your own device. Nothing you enter is uploaded to a server. Clients, periods, mappings, books and drafts are saved in that browser only. The top bar shows when your work was last saved, and a warning appears if the browser refuses to save.

**Back up everything** (in Clients) downloads one file with every client, period, mapping, adjustment, disclosure, the books and attached auditor's reports. **Restore a backup** brings it all back on any device.

The app, its fonts and its PDF and Excel tools are all served from this site, so no other company is contacted when you use it. Anonymous visit counts (GoatCounter: no cookies, no IP addresses stored) are sent only if the user allows them, and never from browsers set to Do Not Track. Details are in the app's manual under Privacy and your data.

### Replacing a device or restoring a paid workspace

- **New computer, phone or a cleared browser:** back up everything on the old device and restore it on the new one. Acivaz lists the paid companies it finds and prepares a WhatsApp request. Replacement codes for the paid time that remains are free, within one working day.
- **Lost or broken device with no backup:** send the MoMo transaction ID and the company name on WhatsApp. Paid time is restored on the new device's company ID. Figures can only come back from a backup.
- **One device at a time:** a replacement code moves the licence; it does not add a second device.

## Install

In Chrome or Edge, choose **Install app**. On a phone, use **Add to Home Screen**. After the first visit it works offline.

## Trial balance format

One row per account: account code, account name, this period's balance, and optionally a comparative, a budget and opening balances (needed for the cash flow statement). Debits positive, credits negative; separate Debit and Credit columns also work. Amounts can be written as `1,234,567.89`, `1.234.567,89`, `1 234 567`, `(1,234)`, `1,234-`, `1,234 Cr` or `RWF 1,234`. Use year-to-date balances before closing entries.

Statements prepared in Acivaz are management-prepared and unaudited until an auditor reports on them. Check every figure before you send them.

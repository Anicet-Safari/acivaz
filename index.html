Help

Acivaz manual

How to keep the books, build a management pack, and run the financial models.

Getting started

Acivaz takes a business from its day-to-day transactions to a board-ready management pack, then on to forecasts, a budget, a valuation, loan or project appraisal and a full business plan. The top bar has five entries: Books, Data (upload a trial balance, then mapping), Reports (the management report and the financial statements), Plan (forecast, budget, valuation, loans and projects, and the business plan) and Manual. Data, Reports and Plan open a short menu; the step you are on shows beside the menu's name.

How the figures flow

There are two ways in. If you keep the client's books in Acivaz, start at Books and press Build management pack when you're ready. If the books are kept in other software, start at Data and upload a trial balance.

Both ways open on example data, a fictional bakery and a fictional maize processor, so you can see every screen working before you enter your own figures.

Your data stays on your device. Files you load and books you keep are processed in your browser and never uploaded. The flip side is that clearing your browser data erases them, so back up your books regularly (see ).

1 · Books

The Books step is a complete set of double-entry books. You fill in plain forms and Acivaz posts the debits and credits for you. Its sections run along the second row of tabs: Overview, Dashboard, Sales, Purchases, Bank, All entries, VAT, Reports and Setup.

The Overview shows cash, what customers owe, what you owe suppliers, profit before tax this year, VAT payable and estimated income tax, all as at the date you choose. Below them are buttons for every type of transaction, a chart of income and expenses by month, and the latest entries.

Dashboard

The Dashboard shows the year so far at a glance, as at the date you choose. Four tiles give revenue, gross margin, profit before tax and cash, each with a small line of the monthly trend. Below them:

  • Cash River: cash in the bank, MoMo and petty cash drawn as a river whose width is the balance at each month end. Money from customers and lenders flows in from above; payments to suppliers, staff, RRA and the bank leave below. Where the river turns red, cash fell below one month of average outgoings. A written takeaway names the tight months and the heaviest month for payments.
  • Revenue and profit by month and Margins by month, so a weak month stands out.
  • Cash at each month end, for the bank, MoMo and petty cash accounts and in total.
  • Who owes what, by age: what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers, split into not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days overdue.
  • Top customers by sales, with what each still owes, and the biggest costs as a share of revenue.

The VAT and tax tracker at the bottom shows output VAT, input VAT and VAT paid to RRA for each month, with the amount owed at each month end. Next to it, income tax due on the year so far, under the company's regime and rate, is drawn against the tax provided in the books. If they differ, press Post provision to bring the provision up to date.

The green check Debits equal credits confirms the books balance. A warning appears if opening balances are incomplete or if you haven't backed up recent changes.

Set up a company

  1. Press New company at the top right of Books.
  2. Enter the company name, the month its financial year starts, and the date the books start. Starting on the first day of a financial year, such as 1 January, lets the pack include a cash flow statement.
  3. Tick Registered for VAT if the company is VAT registered, and press Save.

In Setup, also check three tax settings. Income tax regime: tax on profit, or the small business flat rate for turnover up to RWF 20 million. Tax rate on profit: 28% unless the company qualifies for a reduced rate. VAT and PAYE returns: quarterly for turnover up to RWF 200 million and monthly above it; Acivaz picks this from turnover unless you choose.

You can keep books for as many companies as you like. Switch between them with the company list at the top right. Under Setup you can later change the name, financial year, start date, invoice numbering and currency.

Every company starts with a chart of accounts built for Rwandan businesses, including bank, MoMo and petty cash, VAT payable to RRA, PAYE and RSSB, and income tax payable. In Setup → Chart of accounts you can rename accounts, add your own, and choose which line of the management pack each one feeds. Accounts Acivaz relies on, such as Trade receivables and VAT payable, can be renamed but not deleted.

Opening balances

Opening balances carry over the balance sheet from last year's accounts. They're dated the day before the books start.

  1. Go to Setup and press Enter opening balances.
  2. Enter each balance from last year's balance sheet: debit for assets, credit for liabilities and equity. Leave out trade receivables and trade payables.
  3. Press Unpaid customer invoice for each invoice customers still owed at the start, and Unpaid supplier bill for each bill you still owed. Entering them one by one lets you record payments against them later.

Until everything is entered, the difference sits in an account called Opening balance equity. When it reaches zero, the check in Setup turns to Complete. If it doesn't, compare your entries with last year's balance sheet.

Sales and customers

To record a sale on credit:

  1. In Sales, press New sales invoice.
  2. Enter the customer. Names you've used before are suggested as you type. The invoice number fills in automatically and the due date is set 30 days after the invoice date; change either if you need to.
  3. Add a line for each item: choose the income account, describe it, enter the amount and choose the VAT treatment. Tick Amounts include VAT if your prices already include VAT.
  4. Check the net, VAT and total at the bottom, then press Save.

When the customer pays, press Receive next to the invoice, or Payment received. Choose the account the money went into, such as the bank or MoMo, and enter the amount and a reference, such as the MoMo transaction ID. Part payments are fine. The invoice shows as Part paid until the rest arrives.

The list shows each invoice's status: Unpaid, Part paid, Overdue or Paid. Below it, What customers owe, by age groups the balances into not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days, so you can see who to chase.

For a cash or MoMo sale with no invoice, use Money in instead.

Acivaz invoices are bookkeeping records, not tax invoices. Under Ministerial Order 006/26/10/TC (September 2026), the invoice a customer receives must come from RRA's EBM or e-invoicing system. Issue it there, then type its receipt number into EBM / e-invoice receipt number on the sale in Acivaz. For a VAT-registered company, a check at the top of Books counts sales this year that have no receipt number.

Bills and suppliers

Purchases works the same way for suppliers. Enter the supplier's EBM invoice number on each bill: RRA only allows input VAT that is backed by one. Press New supplier bill to record a bill you'll pay later, then Pay when you pay it. Choose an expense account for costs, or an asset account such as Property, plant and equipment for equipment. What you owe suppliers, by age shows what's due and when.

Money in, money out, transfers

  • Money in: cash sales, other income, a loan received or capital put in. Choose the account the money went into and the account it relates to.
  • Money out: rent, salaries, utilities, loan repayments, VAT or PAYE paid to RRA, and anything else paid without a bill. One payment can be split over several lines, for example a loan repayment split between the loan and the interest.
  • Transfer: moving money between your own accounts, such as from MoMo to the bank.

All three are in Bank and on the Overview. To change an entry, press Edit. To remove it, press Delete and then Confirm. An invoice or bill with payments against it can only be deleted after its payments.

Journal entries

Use a journal for entries no other form covers: depreciation, accruals, prepayments, reclassifications and corrections. Press Journal entry, then enter each account with an amount in either Debit or Credit. Debits must equal credits before it saves.

For example, monthly depreciation of 420,000: debit 6300 Depreciation 420,000 and credit 1590 Accumulated depreciation 420,000.

All entries lists every transaction, newest first, with a filter by type.

VAT

For a VAT-registered company, every line on an invoice, bill, money in or money out has a VAT treatment:

ChoiceUse it for
18%Standard-rated sales and purchases. VAT is added, or taken out of the amount if it includes VAT.
Zero-ratedSupplies taxed at 0%, such as exports.
ExemptSupplies the law exempts from VAT.
No VATItems outside VAT, such as salaries, loan repayments, bank charges and taxes paid.

Acivaz suggests a treatment from the account you choose; change it whenever it's wrong. Output VAT on sales and input VAT on purchases both go to VAT payable (RRA).

The VAT section summarises any period. Press Last month, This month, Last quarter or This year, or set your own dates. It shows standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt sales, purchases at 18%, and the net VAT payable or refundable. Open Show the lines behind these figures to check every entry. When you pay RRA, press Record VAT payment.

Input VAT on costs that relate to exempt sales may not be claimable. Check before you file the return.

Businesses with turnover up to RWF 200 million declare VAT quarterly and others monthly, within 15 days after the period ends, even when nothing is payable. The VAT section opens on the period that matches the company's filing and shows the due date.

If the company isn't registered, untick Registered for VAT in Setup and no VAT is added anywhere. Registration is required once sales pass RWF 20 million in a year or RWF 5 million in a quarter, and a warning appears at the top of Books when they do.

Income tax

By default Acivaz uses Rwanda's standard corporate income tax rate of 28%. Change the rate in Setup if the company has a reduced rate. For the small business flat rate, there is no tax on annual turnover up to RWF 2 million, then RWF 60,000, 120,000, 210,000 or 300,000 a year up to RWF 12 million, and 3% of turnover up to RWF 20 million. The Overview shows the estimated tax so far, and Reports compares it with the tax already provided in the books. Press Post provision to record the difference as a journal: debit Income tax expense, credit Income tax payable.

The estimate uses accounting profit. Taxable profit can differ, for example through expenses that aren't deductible or tax depreciation, so treat it as a provision rather than the tax return. Quarterly prepayments are due by 30 June, 30 September and 31 December, and the annual return by 31 March. A pack built from the books carries the company's rate into the forecast and the tax note; for an uploaded trial balance, set it under Income tax rate in Pack settings.

Bank reconciliation

  1. In Bank, choose the account by clicking its tile, for example Cash at bank or MoMo.
  2. Under Reconcile with the bank statement, enter the statement date and the closing balance on the statement.
  3. Tick each entry that appears on the statement, in the main list or under Entries not yet ticked.
  4. When Difference to explain reaches zero, the account is reconciled. Press Save reconciliation to keep a record.

If a difference remains, look for entries on the statement that aren't in the books, such as bank charges, and entries recorded with the wrong amount or date.

Reports

Reports shows, as at the date you choose: profit or loss for the year to date, the income tax estimate, the trial balance, and an account ledger with a running balance for any account. Income and expense accounts show the current year; profit from earlier years is included in retained earnings automatically, so you never need closing entries.

Excel at the top of Books downloads the trial balance, day book, sales invoices, supplier bills, VAT summary and chart of accounts.

Backup and restore

Back up after every session. Books are stored in this browser only. Clearing browser data, a new computer or a different browser starts empty.
  1. Press Back up at the top of Books. A file named like Acivaz books backup 2026-10-26.json is saved, containing every company.
  2. Keep it somewhere safe, such as Google Drive or your email.
  3. To restore, press Restore, choose the file, and confirm Replace with backup. This replaces all the books in that browser with the ones in the file.

Backups are always free, during the trial and after it. Use the same steps to move your books to another computer.

From books to pack

Press Build management pack at the top of Books or under the trial balance in Reports. Acivaz turns the books into a trial balance as at your chosen date and opens the Pack with every statement filled in. The comparative column is the same date a year earlier, if the books go back that far. The cash flow statement needs the books to start at the beginning of the financial year, with opening balances entered.

2 · Data

Use Data when the books are kept in other software. Export a trial balance and load it here.

  1. Drop an Excel or CSV file on the box, press Choose file, or copy the rows in Excel and paste them, including the header row, then press Read pasted rows.
  2. Under Match your columns, check the account code, account name, this period's balance and the comparative balance. Budget and opening balances are optional.
  3. Press Use these columns.
  4. Fill in Pack settings: company name, period labels, the number of months covered, and whether to show amounts in millions, thousands or units.

The file needs one row per account. Debits are positive and credits negative, so sales appear as, for example, -612,400,000. If your export shows credits as positive, tick flip all signs.

Use year-to-date balances before closing entries, so that profit is added to equity and the balance sheet ties. For the cash flow statement, add an opening balances column, the trial balance at the start of the year. If your comparative column is last year's closing balance, tick the option to use it as the opening balance sheet instead.

Download template gives you a file in the right layout.

3 · Mapping

Mapping decides which line of the statements each account belongs to, for example Revenue, Cost of sales, Trade receivables or Long-term borrowings. Acivaz maps accounts automatically from their names and codes. Accounts it can't place are highlighted, and the Mapping tab shows how many need attention.

Choose a pack line for any account that needs one, or correct one that's wrong. Tick Show only accounts that need a line to focus on the gaps. Your choices are remembered for that company in this browser, so next month's upload maps itself. Accounts from the Books step are mapped already.

4 · Management report

The management report is what owners and the board read each month. It opens with four checks: the trial balance balances, every account is mapped, the balance sheet ties, and the cash flow reconciles to cash. Fix any check that isn't green before sending the pack.

  • Compare with switches the comparison between the comparative period and the budget.
  • Key figures: revenue, gross margin, net profit and cash.
  • Charts: each line of the results against the comparison, what moved net profit, where each 100 of income went, and how cash moved. In the first chart the comparison sits behind this period as a dashed ghost, so shortfalls show in red and beats in green, and a written takeaway names what lifted and what dragged profit. The takeaway also goes into the PDF.
  • Ratio scorecard: each ratio placed on a band of Healthy, Watch and Act zones, with this period as a dot and the comparative as a ring, so you can see which way it moved.
  • Cost lines per 100 of revenue: every cost account as a share of revenue, this period against the comparative. A red change means that cost took a bigger share. Both also go into the PDF and Excel.
  • Commentary: press Rebuild basic draft for a draft written from the figures, then fill in the notes in square brackets with the real reasons. Edit it freely; it goes into the PDF exactly as written. When Acivaz is opened inside Claude, Draft with Claude writes a fuller version.
  • Ratios: ten ratios with a guide value and a flag of Healthy, Watch or Act.

Results against budget and last year sets each line of the profit or loss against the budget and the comparative, with variances; the formal financial statements show only the period and its comparative, as IFRS for SMEs expects. Fill in Prepared and approved by at the bottom of the report, or of any Plan step, and the PDF ends with signature lines for both, while each Excel workbook opens on a cover sheet with the same names and its contents.

Download report PDF and Excel beside the title download the management report on its own. Download PDF pack and Excel in the top bar download the full pack: the management report and the financial statements together.

5 · Financial statements

The statements for an auditor, RRA, RDB or a bank, laid out in the IFRS for SMEs format and marked unaudited. They use the same figures and checks as the management report.

  • The four statements: profit or loss, financial position, changes in equity, and cash flows using the indirect method, with note references. Costs are shown in brackets, assets and liabilities are split into non-current and current, and every statement uses the same columns so the figures line up from one to the next.
  • Notes: the basis of preparation (editable; Reset to standard wording restores it), account-level breakdowns of each line, the property and equipment movement, and the effective tax rate.
  • Significant accounting policies: a note written for the lines in these statements (revenue, property and equipment, inventories, receivables, borrowings, tax). Press Edit the standard wording to change it.
  • Year-end disclosures and approval: going concern, risks and uncertainties, related party transactions, events after the reporting date, and contingencies and commitments. Each one you fill in becomes a note. Use standard wording inserts a nil statement; use it only when it is true. Suggest from the figures drafts the risks note from liquidity, borrowing, credit and trading trends.

Company law: Law N° 007/2021 governing companies

  • Directors' report (Article 89): principal activities, a review of the business, the dividend and any transfer to reserves, donations, and entries in the register of directors' interests. Left blank, the review is written from the figures; other blank items show "To be completed". A public company must include its principal activities.
  • Signing (Articles 89 and 125): the statements and the report are signed by two directors, or by the only director. A red chip shows if only one is named.
  • Dividend solvency test (Article 69): enter a recommended dividend and Acivaz checks that, after paying it, assets still exceed liabilities, current assets still cover current liabilities, and cash covers it. If it passes, the PDF adds a certificate for the directors to sign; a private company whose shareholders have opted out (Article 209) can leave it out. A dividend note explains that it is not a liability until approved.
  • Filing deadlines (Articles 88, 142 and 143): signed accounts, the auditor's report and the directors' report go to shareholders and the Registrar General within 7 months of the year end for a private company, or 4 months for a public one. Set the year end and the annual return month, and the chips count down. Late filing can cost each director RWF 1 million to 10 million (Article 332).

Download statements PDF gives a cover, contents, the directors' report, the solvency certificate when a dividend passes the test, the approval page, each statement on its own page, and the notes. These are management-prepared statements for the board and the auditor; every company needs an external audit unless a Minister's order exempts it (Articles 131 and 210).

6 · Forecast

A three-year forecast of the profit or loss, balance sheet and cash flow, starting from this year's actuals scaled up to a full year. Every assumption starts from the company's own figures, such as growth, gross margin and debtor days. Change any of them and the forecast updates as you type. Reset from actuals puts them back.

Choose a Scenario: Base, Downside or Upside. The downside and upside cases change growth, gross margin and debtor days by the amounts you set at the bottom of the assumptions. The chart of closing cash shows all three cases together, and a warning appears if cash goes negative in any year, which signals a funding need.

What drives profit breaks the first forecast year's net profit into its lines, each branch as thick as the money it carries. Move a slider to try a change in growth, gross margin, staff or admin cost growth, selling costs or the interest rate: the tree recolours, the panel ranks the effect of each change, and the takeaway shows the new profit and closing cash. Nothing changes in the forecast until you press Use in forecast.

7 · Budget

The budget spreads the first forecast year over twelve months, using the scenario chosen in the Forecast. Choose the month the financial year starts, then give each month a weight for how much of the year's revenue falls in it. Cost of sales, selling costs and other income follow revenue; staff, administration, depreciation, interest and tax are spread evenly. Spread evenly resets the weights.

8 · Valuation

A discounted cash flow valuation built on the forecast. Set the discount rate (WACC), meaning the return lenders and investors expect from a business of this size and risk, and the long-term growth after year 3.

The result shows enterprise value, equity value after deducting borrowings less cash, the share of value coming from after year 3, and value as a multiple of EBITDA. The sensitivity table shows equity value across a range of discount and growth rates. With a three-year forecast, most of the value usually comes from after year 3, so read the result as a range rather than a price.

9 · Loans and projects

Loan repayment schedule: enter the amount, interest rate, term in months and any interest-only grace period, then choose equal monthly instalments or equal principal repayments. It shows the instalment, total interest, a year-by-year summary and the full monthly schedule.

Investment appraisal: enter the initial investment, useful life, the net cash inflow in year 1, how fast it grows, the value at the end of its life, and the discount rate. It shows net present value, internal rate of return, payback, discounted payback and the profitability index. A positive NPV means the project earns more than the discount rate.

Steps 6 to 10 each have their own Excel and Download PDF buttons. Tick Also add the forecast, budget, valuation, and loans and projects to include them in the full pack downloads.

10 · Business plan

A full business plan of thirteen sections, the shape banks, the Business Development Fund and investors expect. The figures come from your accounts and forecast; you write the story around them.

  • Sections 2 to 8 (the business, products, market, competition, marketing, operations, team) are yours to write. Insert starter text adds a paragraph with the points to cover in square brackets; replace each bracket with the real detail.
  • Executive summary: write it last, or press Draft from the figures. It takes the first sentences of your other sections and adds revenue, growth, profit, the funding sought and how it will be repaid. Undo brings back what you had.
  • Financial performance to date and Financial plan are built for you: results against the comparative, key assumptions, projected profit or loss, financial position and cash flows for three years, key ratios, break-even revenue and the three scenarios. Change them through Plan, Forecast; the scenario switch at the top applies here too.
  • Funding request: the amount, its form (loan, equity, grant or a mix) and what it pays for. For a loan, Acivaz checks the debt service cover each year for the amount sought, at the interest rate and term set in Loans and projects. For a mix, enter how much of it is loan. For equity, it shows the share an investor would get at the value from Valuation.
  • Risks and Milestones are short tables. Add risks suggested by the figures adds risks the numbers point to, such as cash running out in the downside case or slow-paying customers, each with a way to manage it.

Milestones take a start month, an end month and who leads each one, and Acivaz draws them as a Gantt chart: on screen, in the PDF above the milestone table, and as a shaded month-by-month timeline in Excel.

The list on the left shows which sections are ready, have gaps in brackets, or are still to write. Download PDF gives a plan with a cover, contents and every section; Excel gives the text, funding, risks, milestones and forecast. Your text is saved on this device for each company.

Install and offline use

Acivaz can be installed like an app. In Chrome or Edge on a computer, press Install app in the top bar. On a phone, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen. It then opens from its own icon, in its own window, and works offline after the first visit.

How Acivaz is built

Acivaz is a single web page that runs entirely in the browser. Nothing a customer enters is sent to a server, which keeps client data private and means there are no hosting costs beyond the free GitHub Pages site.

Because everything lives in the browser, two limits follow: clearing browser data erases the books unless they're backed up, and the free trial restarts if someone clears it. Proper user accounts would need a server, which belongs in a later paid version.

Problems and questions

The trial balance doesn't balance

Check that the file includes every account, including retained earnings, and that there are no subtotal rows. If the signs are reversed, tick flip all signs in Data. A pack built from Books always balances.

The balance sheet doesn't tie

Usually an account is mapped to an income or expense line when it belongs on the balance sheet, or the reverse. Check Mapping, and make sure the balances are year to date and before closing entries.

There is no cash flow statement

It needs an opening balance sheet. In Data, add an opening balances column. In Books, start the books at the beginning of the financial year and enter opening balances.

My books have disappeared

Books live in the browser where you entered them. Check you're using the same browser and computer. If browser data was cleared, press Restore and choose your latest backup.

My unlock code doesn't work

Pack settings

Format. One row per account with an account code, an account name, a balance for this period and a comparative balance. A budget column is optional. Debits are positive and credits negative, for example sales as -612,400,000.

Use year-to-date balances for both periods, before closing entries. The profit for the period is then added to equity and the balance sheet ties. Months covered is used for debtor, creditor and inventory days.

For the cash flow statement, add an opening-balance column: the trial balance at the start of the financial year, after closing entries. Income and expense accounts are blank in that column.
